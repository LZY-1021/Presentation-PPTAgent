## Shanghai Culture: Stone-Gate Dialogues

The Shikumen (Stone-Gate) houses, emerging in 1860s, feature Western-style arched gateways with Chinese courtyard layouts.

- **Xintiandi's renovation**:  
  Preserved 92% original facades  
  Created $1.2B commercial hub  

- **Tianzifang's alleys**:  
  Host over 200 artists' studios  
  Coexist with traditional wet markets
