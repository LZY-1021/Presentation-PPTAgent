## 1930年代：外滩的黄金时代

By 1930s, the Bund's neoclassical buildings stood like a pearl necklace along the Huangpu River.  
The Shikumen alleyways witnessed both the glamour of cheongsam-clad socialites and the birth of the Communist Party in 1921 (Site: 76 Xingye Road).
